About Stone Lake Retreat

A new chapter for a beloved retreat

Our Story

A Place Worth Sharing

Nestled on five peaceful acres in Saylorsburg, Pennsylvania, Stone Lake Retreat has been welcoming guests to the Pocono Mountains for years. When we first discovered this property—with its charming farmhouse, private two-acre lake, and that iconic red boat dock—we knew it was something special.

After a brief hiatus, we're excited to reopen the inn and share this slice of Pocono paradise with travelers seeking something beyond the ordinary. Whether you're here for adventure, relaxation, or a bit of both, Stone Lake Retreat offers a genuine escape from everyday life.

"Some places just feel like home the moment you arrive."

Each of our five guest rooms has been thoughtfully appointed with its own character—from the serene Emerald Room to the whimsical Aviator Room. We've preserved the property's rustic charm while ensuring modern comforts for today's travelers.

Yes, our private two-acre lake is perfect for swimming. It's a natural lake with a gentle slope, making it great for all ages. We also have kayaks and paddle boards available for guests. As always, swim at your own risk and supervise children at all times.

Saylorsburg is just a few minutes away with basic amenities. Stroudsburg, a charming downtown with restaurants, shops, and services, is about 15 minutes. The Crossings Premium Outlets are also nearby for shopping.

Pegasus Airpark is a small private airport that's home to Pegasus Skydive Center. You'll see small planes and skydivers throughout the day (weather permitting). It's more charming than disruptive—many guests love watching from the porch! If you're feeling adventurous, you can walk across the street and book a tandem jump.

Please contact us to discuss bringing pets. We may be able to accommodate well-behaved dogs depending on the situation and other guest bookings.
