Our Accommodations

Five distinctive rooms, each with its own character and charm

Each of our five guest rooms has been thoughtfully designed with its own unique personality. From the serene greens of the Emerald Room to the whimsical aviation decor of the Aviator Room, you'll find a space that speaks to you.

The Emerald Room

Our most spacious retreat

Bathed in soothing sage and forest green tones, the Emerald Room is our largest and most luxurious accommodation. The room features a cozy electric fireplace, plush queen bed, and private en-suite bathroom—perfect for those seeking extra space and privacy.

Large windows overlook the property, filling the room with natural light during the day and offering peaceful views of the grounds.

The Aviator Room

Where dreams take flight

A truly unique space filled with aviation memorabilia and vintage-inspired decor. Model planes, propellers, and flight maps adorn the walls, creating an immersive experience that delights guests of all ages—especially kids.

Fitting, since Pegasus Airpark and Skydive Center sits just across the road. Watch small planes take off and land from nearby windows, or walk over to experience skydiving yourself!

The Wine Room

Rustic elegance

Step into warmth with walls crafted from beautiful reclaimed wood planks. The Wine Room features rich burgundy accents, industrial-style pendant lighting, and a cozy atmosphere that feels like a sophisticated cabin retreat.

A pedestal sink and vintage-inspired furnishings complete the aesthetic, making this room perfect for couples seeking a romantic getaway.
