Booking Policies

Check-In / Check-Out

Check-in is at 3:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request.

Cancellation

Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before arrival. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to a one-night charge.

Guests

Maximum occupancy varies by room. Please inquire for groups larger than 10 guests or whole-house rentals.

Pets

Please inquire about pet policies. Some restrictions may apply.

Smoking

Stone Lake Retreat is a non-smoking property. Smoking is only permitted in designated outdoor areas.

Payment

A deposit may be required to confirm your booking. Final payment details will be provided upon confirmation.
