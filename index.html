Welcome

A Storied Retreat in the Poconos

Nestled on five peaceful acres in Saylorsburg, Pennsylvania, Stone Lake Retreat offers the perfect blend of rustic charm and modern comfort.

Our classic farmhouse, with its welcoming red door, overlooks a two-acre private lake surrounded by rolling hills and seasonal beauty. Whether you're seeking adventure or simply a place to unwind, you'll find it here.
