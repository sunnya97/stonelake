Private Lake

The crown jewel of Stone Lake Inn is our two-acre private lake. Crystal clear and completely yours to enjoy during your stay, the lake offers a peaceful escape from everyday life.

Our charming red covered boat dock provides the perfect spot for morning coffee, afternoon reading, or evening stargazing.

Swimming Fishing Kayaking Paddle Boarding

Hot Tub & Sauna

After a day of adventure—whether skiing, rafting, or paintballing—nothing beats sinking into our outdoor hot tub. With views of the property and stars overhead, it's the perfect way to unwind.

Our private sauna offers another way to relax and rejuvenate during your stay.

Outdoor Hot Tub Private Sauna Year-Round

Fire Pit & Outdoor Spaces

Gather around the fire pit for s'mores, stories, and starlit conversations. Our expansive outdoor spaces include comfortable seating areas, a BBQ grill, and a large grass field perfect for lawn games.

The property truly comes alive in fall when the surrounding foliage transforms into a stunning display of reds, oranges, and golds.

Fire Pit BBQ Grill Outdoor Seating Large Lawn

Skydiving at Your Doorstep

Directly across the road sits Pegasus Airpark, home to Pegasus Skydive Center—one of the premier skydiving facilities in the Northeast. Watch small planes take off and skydivers descend right from our property.

Feeling adventurous? Walk across the street for a tandem jump with experienced instructors. It's an unforgettable addition to any Pocono getaway.
