Adventure

Thrill Seekers Welcome

The Poconos are a haven for adventure. From skydiving across the street to world-class paintball and whitewater rapids.

Across the Street Year-Round

Pegasus Skydive Center

Located literally across the road from Stone Lake Retreat, Pegasus Skydive Center offers tandem skydiving with experienced instructors. Even if you're not jumping, watching skydivers descend from our property is an experience in itself.

Their staff has over 87,000 jumps combined. First-timers welcome!

Visit Website
~20 min drive Year-Round

Skirmish USA Paintball

The world's largest paintball facility spans over 700 acres with 50+ unique playing fields. From dense woods and castles to tanks and airplanes, Skirmish offers terrain for every style of play.

Perfect for bachelor parties, team building, or a day of epic fun with friends. All equipment provided.

Visit Website
~30 min drive Spring-Fall

Lehigh River Whitewater Rafting

Pennsylvania's best whitewater rafting experience. The Lehigh River offers Class II-III rapids through the stunning Lehigh Gorge, with 12+ miles of scenic adventure.

Multiple outfitters offer guided trips ranging from family-friendly floats to dam-release adventures for thrill seekers.

Explore

Small Town Charm

Discover the unique character of the Poconos' most charming towns, just minutes from your doorstep.

~15 min drive

Stroudsburg

A vibrant, walkable downtown packed with cozy cafes, craft coffee shops, and diverse restaurants - from farm-to-table American to Thai, Italian, and Mexican. Live music venues, local boutiques, and seasonal festivals.

~30 min drive

Jim Thorpe

Known as the "Switzerland of America," this Victorian-era town offers stunning architecture, unique shops, galleries, and excellent restaurants. Magical during fall foliage season.

Wine Country

Pocono Wine Trail

Eight family-owned wineries wind through the beautiful Pocono Mountains. Pennsylvania is the fifth largest grape producer in America, and the region's unique climate produces exceptional wines.

Cherry Valley Vineyards

Since 1981 - over 100 awards for their wines. One of the Poconos' original wineries.

Tolino Vineyards

Governor's Cup winner - their Cabernet Franc was named Best Dry Grape Wine in Pennsylvania.

Mountain View Vineyard

The only winery on the trail that's also a distillery. Wine, spirits, and stunning views.

Big Creek Vineyard

Family-owned since 1996. White, rosé, red, and sweet fruit wines.

Blue Ridge Vineyard

17,000 sq ft tasting facility with wrap-around deck overlooking the vineyards.

Pocono Beverage Trail

Explore wineries, breweries, and distilleries with one convenient trail passport.

Winter

Ski Season Awaits

Two major ski resorts just 20 minutes away make Stone Lake Retreat the perfect base for winter adventures.

Pennsylvania's highest vertical at 1,082 feet with 40 trails. Excellent night skiing and snowmaking. Great for all skill levels.

~20 minutes away
